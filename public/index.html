How these numbers are calculated
  • Net revenue: Shopify's current order total after edits, refunds and returns. Includes GST and shipping. Cancelled and test orders excluded. Dated by order date (IST).
  • Ex-GST: net revenue minus the order's current tax.
  • Gross: original checkout total of every order placed, including ones later cancelled.
  • New vs repeat: an order is "new" if it's that customer's first non-cancelled order ever.
  • COD: orders whose payment gateway name matches COD.
  • Won't match Shopify Analytics to the rupee: Shopify books refunds on the refund date; this books them against the original order date. COD orders returned as RTO only drop out once they're cancelled in Shopify.
